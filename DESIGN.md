# Canvas Cloud visual identity

Canvas Cloud is the violet member of the BlueCloud office family. Its logo uses the family’s black-and-off-white engraved illustration over a vivid solid color, with a squircle silhouette and transparent outer corners. A drawing board, connected shapes, sticky note, and pencil represent freeform thinking.

The application uses a quiet white and pale-violet workspace, subtle borders, a dotted infinite canvas, and violet selection controls. The board is the focus: a compact tool palette, a device-local board list on the left, and a contextual inspector on the right. No gradients, glass, marketing statistics, or decorative animation.

Tokens: accent #7950ca; text #282332; workspace #faf9fc; surfaces white; border #e9e6ed. UI type uses the installed system sans-serif family, without remote font requests. Standard spacing is 8/12/16/24/32 pixels. Corners are 8–13 pixels for controls and palettes.

Desktop is the primary editing experience. The inspector hides below 900px and the board list below 650px; drawing and double-click text editing remain available. Controls have accessible names, visible keyboard focus, and documented shortcuts. Motion is limited to short color/opacity feedback and respects reduced-motion preferences.
